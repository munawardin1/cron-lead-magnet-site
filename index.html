Our main feature

We tell Meta who bought.

Today, Meta only knows that someone sent you a message. It never finds out if that person bought or not. So it keeps showing your ads to people who just chat.

Cron Meta Lead Manager sends that feedback to Meta for you. When your team marks a chat as bought or not interested, Meta learns the difference, and starts showing your ads to more people like your real buyers.

How it works

Your team marks the lead. Meta learns who buys.

Each chat moves through simple stages. When a salesman taps one, Cron Meta Lead Manager sends the matching event to Meta through the Conversions API, credited to the exact ad the customer clicked.

Over time Meta stops showing your ads to people who only ask the price, and shows them to people who look like your buyers.

    FAQ

    Questions business owners ask us.

    Do I need to change my WhatsApp number?

    No. You can link the phone you already use with a QR code and keep replying from it. If you want your team to reply from Cron Meta Lead Manager, we move the number to the official WhatsApp Business API with you, step by step, and tell you exactly what changes before anything is touched.

    How does Meta learn from my sales?

    When a salesman marks a lead as Qualified, Visit booked or Won, Cron Meta Lead Manager sends that event to your Meta dataset through the Conversions API, linked to the ad the customer clicked. Meta then uses these real results to show your ads to similar people.

    How long does setup take?

    The setup itself is a few taps and usually under an hour on a call with us. Before that, Meta checks your business, which can take a few days.

    Is my customer data safe?

    Each business has its own separate workspace, and only your team can log in to it. We never sell or share your data. For full separation, the Dedicated plan runs on its own server.

    Does it work with Instagram ads?

    Yes, when the ad's button opens WhatsApp. Facebook and Instagram ads that use "Send WhatsApp message" are tracked the same way. Ads that open Instagram Direct are coming soon.

    Which countries and languages do you support?

    Any country where WhatsApp ads run, including the UAE, Saudi Arabia, the wider Gulf, the UK and Pakistan. Chats can be in Arabic, English, Urdu or any other language, and reports use your own currency.

    Is Cron Meta Lead Manager made by Meta?

    No. Cron Meta Lead Manager is built by Cron Technologies and works with Meta's official tools, the WhatsApp Business API and the Conversions API.
